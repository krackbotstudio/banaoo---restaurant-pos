# Kitchen display

The **Kitchen** page is your kitchen display system (KDS) for the active outlet.

## What you see

- New orders with order number, time, table or type, and line items.
- **Online** orders show a platform badge when sourced from integrations.
- Item lines respect status (pending, preparing, ready, cancelled).

## Order actions

Typical flow:

1. **Pending / Preparing** → mark as cooking or advance status.
2. **Ready** → food is ready for pickup or service.
3. Completed orders leave the kitchen view.

Exact button labels depend on your workflow configuration.

## Sound alerts

- Toggle **sound on/off** on the Kitchen page.
- On first visit, tap anywhere once so the browser allows notification sounds (browser policy).
- New online orders can trigger a ringtone when sound is enabled.

## Printing from kitchen

- **Print** on an order sends a kitchen ticket (KOT) to configured kitchen printers.
- In self-service outlets, online orders may auto-print when **auto print** is enabled on the kitchen printer.
- Uses the same routing as POS: fleet printers first, then native/legacy printer settings on the Android app.

## Self-service note

Self-service mode may print a **kitchen handoff** slip (items + quantities) on the **billing** printer as a second job after the customer bill. Kitchen station copy is optional via printer settings.
