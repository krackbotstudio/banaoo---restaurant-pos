# Getting started with banaoo

banaoo is a cloud POS and restaurant management platform for Indian restaurants. You can run orders, kitchen, billing, staff, and analytics from the web dashboard or the Android app.

## 1. Create your account

1. Go to [Get Started](/auth) and sign up with your email or Google.
2. Complete onboarding: restaurant name, contact details, and your first outlet.
3. Choose your plan on the **Pricing** page if prompted.

## 2. Add your first outlet

An **outlet** is one physical location (branch). Each outlet has its own menu, staff, printers, and operation mode.

- Open **Outlets** from the dashboard.
- Add outlet name, address, GST (optional), and **operation mode** (standard counter, self-service, etc.).

## 3. Build your menu

1. Open **Menu** for the active outlet.
2. Create **categories** (e.g. Starters, Mains, Beverages).
3. Add **items** with price, GST rate, and availability.
4. Use modifiers or add-ons where needed.

Tip: The **QR Menu Builder** tool on the landing site lets you create a shareable digital menu without a full POS setup.

## 4. Invite staff

1. Go to **Staff** and add team members with roles (owner, manager, cashier, kitchen).
2. Staff can sign in at **Staff login** with the PIN or credentials you assign.
3. Limit access by outlet where supported.

## 5. Configure printers (recommended)

Thermal printers power kitchen tickets and customer bills. See [Printers](/docs/printers) and [Mobile app](/docs/mobile-app) if you use Bluetooth or the Android APK.

## 6. Take your first order

- **Orders → New order** for counter POS.
- Send items to **Kitchen** automatically if auto-print is enabled.
- Complete payment and print the bill from the order screen.
