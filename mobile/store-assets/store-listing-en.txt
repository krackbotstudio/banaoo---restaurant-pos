Flaze is the all-in-one restaurant POS and management app built for Indian food businesses.

Run service from one app on your Android device:
• Take and manage dine-in, takeaway, and delivery orders
• Kitchen display workflow — see and update order status in real time
• Staff-friendly interface matching the Flaze web POS
• Bluetooth thermal printing for kitchen tickets and customer bills
• Multi-outlet support for growing brands
• Secure sign-in with your Flaze account

The mobile app wraps the same Flaze POS you use on the web, so your team gets a consistent experience with native Bluetooth printer support for ESC/POS devices.
